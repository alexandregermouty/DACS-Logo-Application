# User guide

The tool runs in the browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox or Safari, recent versions). Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded. Choose your role with the button at the top right (**Role and settings**).

## UNESCO Secretariat

### Start a session
- **First time, or when new Form responses have arrived:** *Open the request register* and choose `DACS_Logo_Requests_Register_2026.xlsx` (download it from SharePoint first). New Form IDs are added; submission data are refreshed; what you recorded in the tool is kept.
- **Later sessions:** *Continue from a workspace file* and open the last `DACS-logo-workspace_*.json`.
- Enter your name in *Role and settings*: it is recorded in the history of each request.

### Assess a request (*Requests* → click a row)
1. **Submission.** The applicant's statement of record, as submitted. Read-only.
2. **Screening.** Assigned reviewer, acknowledgement, completeness (including the signed commitment letter). Answer the four **hard gates**: one "Yes" gives *BLOCK* (refusal, whatever the merits); one "To verify" gives *HOLD* (revert to the applicant before the score means anything).
3. **Assessment.** Retain the **level** (the level requested by the applicant is not binding), the primary overarching challenge and the **main goals** the activity actually serves (✓ marks those the applicant declared). Score the **six criteria** from 0 to 5; the weighted score and the recommendation update as you go. Explain any departure from the band in the *Reviewer notes*.
4. **Working Group consultation.** Select the Working Groups to consult; the tool suggests them from the main goals retained. MG5.2 and MG5.3 alone: consult WG5 only if the request is borderline, high-profile, or a hard gate is on HOLD.
5. **Decision.** Final decision, date, conditions, authorisation period (never open-ended), notification. At Level 2 the SMC decides and at Level 3 the ESC: their decision is filled in automatically when you import their response.
6. **Follow-up.** Status, owner, next review date, follow-up action. The history lists every change.

The side panel shows the weighted score against the decision bands and the **indicative timeline** in working days (target: about 40 working days for a complete Level 1 request).

*Print assessment sheet* produces a clean record of the request for the file.

### Consult the Working Groups, the SMC and the ESC (*Review packs & files*)
- **A. Send a review pack.** Choose the recipient. Requests referred to that body and not yet answered are preselected. Set the response date (10 working days for a Working Group opinion) and a message. Leave *Include the applicant's name and email* unticked unless the reviewer needs them. Download the pack and send it by email or SharePoint. For a Working Group, the *Sent to WG* date and the status *3 - With WG* are set.
- **B. Import responses.** Open one or several response files. Check the preview, then *Apply*.
- **C. Workspace.** *Save workspace file* at the end of each session, and store it on SharePoint next to the register. The header shows *Unsaved changes* until you do.
- **D. Back to Excel.** *Register update* to paste into the register (instructions on its first sheet); *Summary* for meetings.

Never put the register, a workspace, a pack or a response file in the GitHub repository: they contain personal data.

## Working Group leadership, SMC and ESC members

1. Open the tool and choose *Open a review pack*. Select the `.json` file received from the Secretariat. Your role is set automatically from the pack.
2. *Requests for your review* lists the requests referred to you. Open each one: the submission, the Secretariat's screening, scores and notes, and any opinion already given are shown read-only.
3. In the framed panel on the right, choose your opinion or decision, write your reasons and, if needed, the conditions you propose. Your inputs are kept in this browser as you type.
   - **Working Group, Level 1:** concurrence with the Secretariat's decision.
   - **Working Group, Levels 2 and 3:** a substantive written opinion on the claimed contribution to the main goals.
   - **SMC, Level 2:** decision to endorse (with or without conditions), not to endorse, or to defer.
   - **SMC, Level 3:** recommendation to the ESC.
   - **ESC, Level 3:** decision on the flagship designation.
4. Go to *My response*, enter your name and function, and *Download response file*. Send it back to the Secretariat. You may send your answers in several instalments.

On a shared computer, open *Role and settings* and choose *Remove everything kept in this browser* when you have finished.
